## Design moves worth stealing from mynrd.co.uk
1. **Let the work carry it** — minimal copy on the cards, big confident visuals. (We're close; I'd trim card text further.)
2. **Identity in the nav**, hero stays relaxed. (Done above.)
3. **A belief line** that gives you a POV without a résumé. (Added.)
4. Keep your **color + character** though — that's your edge over his neutral gallery. Don't go grayscale.
